How the Tracker Works

Our live stock tracker connects directly to the Grow a Garden 2 seed shop and monitors inventory changes in real time. The tracker polls for new data, so you will see stock updates within moments of a restock occurring in the game.

Each item card displays the item name, current quantity available, rarity tier, and the shop price. Items are color-coded by rarity — from Common all the way up to Divine — making it easy to spot valuable finds at a glance. Only items currently in stock are displayed, so you can focus on what is available right now.

The tracker covers the main shop categories:

Seeds — The main seed shop inventory including all rarity tiers from Common Carrot to Super Moon Bloom.

Gear — Tools like Watering Can, Shovel, Sprinkler, Trowel, and rare equipment.

Crates — Buildable items including Fence, Bridge, Light, Teleporter, and more.

Seed Packs — Bundled seed packages with multiple seeds at special prices.

How to Set Up Alerts

Missing a Beanstalk, Moon Bloom, or other high-rarity seed because you were not watching the shop is one of the most common frustrations in Grow a Garden 2. Our built-in sound alert system solves this problem completely.

Here is how to set up rare seed notifications:

Click the Alerts button at the top of the page to open the notification settings panel.

Enable Sound Alerts using the master toggle switch at the top of the panel.

Select rarity tiers you want to track. By default, Legendary and above are tracked.

Adjust the volume and use the Test button to preview the alert chime.

Keep this tab open in your browser. When a tracked item appears in stock, you will hear the notification instantly.

This is especially valuable for Super, Mythic, Prismatic, and Divine rarity items that appear very rarely in restocks. Your preferences are saved locally and persist between sessions — no account needed.

FAQ

How often does the Grow a Garden 2 seed shop restock?
The seed shop restocks every 5 in-game minutes (which corresponds to 5 real-time minutes). Restock timers are displayed dynamically on our dashboard for Seeds, Gears, Crates, and Auction lots.
How does the live stock tracker work?
Our automated Roblox scraper runs in the background of active game servers and securely uploads stock values, weather states, and fruit price multipliers to our central server whenever a change is detected.
Can I get sound notifications for rare seeds?
Yes! By clicking the bell icon at the top of the tracker dashboard, you can enable browser sound notifications for Legendary, Mythic, Prismatic, and Divine seed types.
What do the rarity colors mean?
Colors highlight seed and gear rarity. Common is gray, Uncommon is green, Rare is blue, Epic is purple, Legendary is orange, Mythic is red, and Divine is light blue/rainbow. Higher rarity seeds yield crops that sell for exponentially more coins.
Is the stock the same on every server?
Yes, the standard seed shop, gear shop, and crate shop stocks are synchronized globally across all public game servers. However, the custom auction house items and prices might differ if players create private bidding pools.
Does this tracker work on mobile devices?
Absolutely! The tracker layout is fully responsive and optimized for mobile screens. You can check live stocks, predictions, and weather trends on Android and iOS devices.
